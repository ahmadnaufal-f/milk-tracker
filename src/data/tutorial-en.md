Welcome! Follow these simple steps to start tracking your breastfeeding journey:

---

### 1. Login to the App
Before starting, make sure you have logged in using your Google account:
![Login with Google](./login-google.webp)
* Tap the **"Login with Google"** button on the home page.
* This step is important so that your record data is stored safely and not lost.

---

### 2. How to Record Pumping Sessions
This application is equipped with an automatic timer:
![Start Pumping](./start-pumping.webp)
1. **Start:** When you are about to pump, tap the **"Start Pumping"** button. The time will start running.
![Stop Pumping](./stop-pumping.webp)
2. **Finish:** When you are done, tap the **"Stop Pumping"** button.
![Save Session](./save-session.webp)
3. **Fill Results:** Enter the total milk obtained in **ml**.
4. **Save:** Click save to enter it into the daily history.

---

### 3. Viewing Record History (Log)
* **Daily Log:** You can see today's list of pumps directly at the **bottom of the main page**.
* **Monthly Log:** To view all records from previous days, tap the **"View All History"** button.

---

### 4. Tips: Install on Home Screen (PWA)
For more practical access like a regular application without opening a browser:
![Add to Home Screen](./add-to-home-screen.webp)
* **Android Users (Chrome):** Tap the **three dots icon (⋮)** in the top right corner, then select **"Install app/Add to Home Screen"**.
* **iPhone Users (Safari):** Tap the **Share** icon (box with upward arrow), then select **"Add to Home Screen"**.

---

### 5. If Milk Tracker shows a move notice
* Finish and save your current session before continuing.
* If you use a guest account, choose **"Move my guest data"** and keep the private, short-lived link to yourself. You do not need a Google account for this.
* If you use Google, open the new site and sign in with the same Google account.
* Check your history and settings on the new site before removing the old app. Add the new site to your home screen again; reminders may need to be enabled there.

---

*Hope your pumping session is pleasant and smooth!*
